About me

MSc Data Science student at Queen Mary University of London (2026–2027), focusing on Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning and Big Data Processing.

I build data and AI systems that hold up in production, from ETL pipelines on Apache Spark and Databricks to machine learning models and LLM-based agents.

My work covers the whole data and ML lifecycle: cleaning and validating data at scale, engineering features, training and evaluating models with PyTorch, TensorFlow and scikit-learn, and building agentic assistants with LangChain and LangGraph, vector memory and FastAPI.

With 2.2 years as an Associate Consultant at EY and internships at Amazon and Village Company, I bring hands-on experience with production data pipelines across 50+ interfaces, Kafka streaming, and ML for compliance in FMCG, retail and oil and gas sectors. I've also published two research papers with IEEE and Springer, and I'm a Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate.
